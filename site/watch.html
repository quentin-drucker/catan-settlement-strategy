Watch a Game

Watching a game lets you see the moves of four greedy CPU players as they draft the highest value spots. Games played using a snake draft and a standard draft happen right next to each other on their respective boards, and the scores are displayed under.

Things to Note

  • The seed can be changed by entering a completely new number in the field or by clicking the “New Board” button.
  • The “Board” dropdown toggles the board type between ‘separated’ and ‘true random’.
  • “Next Move” makes one step through the simulation for each click.
  • “Autoplay” progresses through the whole simulation with small pauses between each selection.
  • “Restart” clears the board of settlements but keeps the current seed.
