      What to look for

      • Every turn, both games take the same spot. Greedy players only look at the board, never at who is picking.
      • Turns 1 to 4 are identical. From turn 5 the orders split: snake gives D two picks in a row, standard goes back to A.
      • Under standard, A collects the best first pick and the best leftover. Under snake, the leftovers flow back towards D.
      • Houses are settlements (letter = player). Grey dots are blocked spots, next to a settlement. The gold ring marks the newest move.

      Replay the same game in the terminal with python main.py play --seed 42 --draft snake.
