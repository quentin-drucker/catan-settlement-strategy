Does a snake draft cancel out the advantage of picking first?

Mostly. Four computer players each draft two settlement spots on a Catan board. Over 100,000 games, the snake order (A B C D D C B A) shrinks the gap between the best and worst seat from 3.64 points under the standard order to 0.19.

Same picks, different owners

With greedy players, both drafts take the same eight spots in the same order. The draft only decides who gets each one. Snake hands the weaker second-round picks back to the seats that picked first.

Results at a glance

  • 0.19 points between the best and worst seat on average, snake draft
  • 3.64 points between the best and worst seat, standard draft
  • 90% of standard-draft games won by seat A (ties split)
  • 4× smaller snake gap when 6s and 8s are kept apart (0.88 to 0.19)
