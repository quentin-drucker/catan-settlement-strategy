Play

Pick two settlement spots against three greedy computer players. A spot is worth the dice weights of the tiles around it, and no spot may sit right next to a settlement.

    Tips

    • Hover over a spot (or turn on "Show spot values") to see what it's worth: tiles 8, 5 and 9 make 5 + 4 + 4 = 13.
    • Taking a spot also blocks its neighbours for everyone, including you. Blocking a strong spot an opponent wants can pay off.
    • Keyboard: Tab moves between open spots on your turn; Enter or Space picks one.
    • The seed is shown above the board; the same seed always gives the same board. Computer moves can differ from a computer-only game once you pick, because ties between equal spots are broken in a different order.
